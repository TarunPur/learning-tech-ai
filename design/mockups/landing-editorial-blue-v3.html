A few questions before you start.

Will NOD use my real names or company details?

Names and identifying details are masked before NOD processes your message, and your saved copy stays masked. Your real names are only filled back into the version you copy to send.

What kind of messages can NOD help with?

Outreach you actually send — a follow-up to a prospect who went quiet, a cold intro, a meeting or demo request, an event follow-up. One real message at a time.

Is this for people who already use AI?

Yes. If a chat tool hands you a draft but you can't tell whether it's any good, NOD is the part that's missing — a fixed standard that tells you what to change, not another quick draft.

How is this different from a template or a generic AI prompt?

A template gives you words to copy. NOD holds your real message to how the best outreach actually works, shows you the one line worth changing and why — and you make the call.

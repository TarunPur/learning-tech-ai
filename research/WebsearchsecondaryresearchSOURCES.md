# Secondary-research source register

Research date: 15 August 2026. Sources are grouped by use; individual answers in `Websearchsecondaryresearch.md` link back to these original pages.

## Workforce, skills, and workplace AI

1. [World Economic Forum — Future of Jobs Report 2025](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/) — employer survey across 1,000+ employers, 55 economies; skills, jobs and transformation.
2. [World Economic Forum — Future of Jobs Report 2025 PDF](https://reports.weforum.org/docs/WEF_Future_of_Jobs_Report_2025.pdf) — underlying report, including skill rankings.
3. [Microsoft — 2025 Work Trend Index executive summary](https://cdn-dynmedia-1.microsoft.com/is/content/microsoftcorp/microsoft/final/en-us/microsoft-product-and-services/ai/pdf/executive-summary-work-trend-index-annual-report.pdf) — 31,000-worker survey and workplace signals.
4. [Microsoft / LinkedIn — AI at Work Is Here. Now Comes the Hard Part](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/ai-at-work-is-here-now-comes-the-hard-part/) — 2024 workplace adoption, training and career signals.
5. [OECD — AI and skills](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/ai-and-skills_f843b352-en/full-report.html) — AI skill needs, training and employer barriers.
6. [OECD — Bridging the AI skills gap](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/bridging-the-ai-skills-gap_66d0702e-en.html) — workforce reskilling and training-supply context.
7. [Coursera — Global Skills Report 2025](https://www.coursera.org/skills-reports/global) — learning trends and country/region variation from 170m+ learners.

## Learning, practice, retention, and training transfer

8. [Shukla (2024) — Factors affecting transfer of online training: systematic review](https://doi.org/10.1002/hrdq.21518) — factors affecting online-training transfer.
9. [Blume et al. (2010) — Transfer of Training: meta-analytic review](https://doi.org/10.1177/0149206309352880) — learner, intervention, and work-environment predictors of transfer.
10. [Ghosh et al. (2017) — Improving transfer of training with transfer design](https://doi.org/10.1108/JWL-08-2016-0079) — role of supervisor support in training transfer.
11. [ACM Learning@Scale — Predictive feedback and MOOC passing rates](https://doi.org/10.1145/3430895.3460991) — timely feedback intervention and course-completion evidence.
12. [EEF — Metacognition and self-regulation evidence review](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/evidence-reviews/metacognition-and-self-regulation) — evidence review on self-regulated learning.
13. [ERIC — Spaced retrieval with real-time learning analytics](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ1290236.pdf) — retrieval practice, feedback, and retention.
14. [Frontline Learning Research — Predicting dropout in professional online learning](https://doi.org/10.14786/flr.v13i4.1351) — professional-learning behavioral signals and dropout; small pilot, so directional only.
15. [Ford, Yelon & Billington — How much is transferred from training to the job?](https://doi.org/10.1002/piq.20108) — cautions against equating completion with transfer; recommends observable transfer objectives.
16. [IEEE — Workplace e-learning: factors affecting perceived transfer](https://doi.org/10.1109/PCI.2010.40) — examines motivation, content validity, trainer communication and supervisor support.
17. [EEF — Micro-randomised trials for rapid evidence](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/news/how-micro-randomised-control-trials-can-generate-rapid-evidence-on-classroom-practice) — practical experimental approach for repeated learning-design choices.

## Limits of this evidence base

- Several sources are vendor-produced reports or product pages; use them to describe their data/methodology or offering, not as neutral proof of product impact.
- Global reports establish broad patterns; they cannot identify the best initial user segment, their exact workflow, or willingness to pay. Those require primary research.
- The case study does not name a target geography. No source can select one without a product decision.

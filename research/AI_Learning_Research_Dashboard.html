Data reading note

Handling multi-select responses

Options are matched against each full survey response. The dashboard does not split blindly on commas, because some valid option labels contain commas.
Custom written responses are retained in the source but excluded from named-option charts unless they exactly match a survey option.

Reading this view

Data discipline

This is descriptive primary research. Differences show how respondents in each stated group answered; they do not establish why those differences exist.
Small groups should be read cautiously—especially the 50+ age group and “Prefer not to say” gender response.
